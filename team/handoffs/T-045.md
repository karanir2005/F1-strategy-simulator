# T-045 ranking statistic + DNF audit (data-scientist)

**Done.** Default order and predicted podium are now by expected FIA points (ties by E[pos]), not mean position. DNF model retuned. Artifacts regenerated, pytest 361 passed (5 new in `backend/tests/test_t045_ranking.py`), `tsc` and `lint` clean (build not run).

**Cause of the Dutch GP complaint.** Not a bug. `simulate()` places DNF cars behind all finishers (ordered by their race performance), which is correct. NOR's mean position was 1.06 when classified, but a 17.5% DNF puts him ~19th, so 0.175 x ~18 adds ~3 places to the mean (4.07). All 8 clear inversions in 41 walk-forward races (2025 R2-24, 2026 R1-18; pairs where A has p_win >= B + 0.10, p_podium >= B and median <= B but worse E[pos]) were DNF-tail cases (5 in 2026, 3 in 2025; worst: R14 NOR/ANT, R16 VER/ANT, VER/HAM). 4 races had a >50% favourite below rank 1 by E[pos] (2025 R3, 2026 R12/14/16). Sanity checks on 855 driver rows: p_win <= p_podium <= p_top10 always; per-race sums exactly 1/3/10; no other defect found. Median position fixes most but ties and still inverts once. Expected points was the only statistic with zero inversions in both seasons and the best Spearman/MAE.
