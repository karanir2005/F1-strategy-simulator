## Phase 1 diagnosis (baseline = T-020 tyre fit)
- Stop count: optimizer says 1 stop in 30/34 races; it matches the top-10 mode in 23/34, but "always 1 stop" gets 21/34, so no real skill. Plan wear never justifies a 2nd stop: borrowing a track's wear from the other era (r about 0.6 across years) changed nothing; pit loss is not the cause (effective in+out-lap loss 22.4 s vs pit-lane 23.2 s, `diag_pitloss.py`).
- 2026 strategies are SC-driven: 91 of 208 top-10 stops (44%) fell under SC/VSC laps (2025: 68/319, 21%). Without SC reaction the optimizer cannot match stop timing (median pit-window error 6 laps on green-only stops). SC-aware ranking (1000 sims) did not help (24 vs 23 of 34).
- Compound mix: optimizer put SOFT in 47% of plan laps (teams 14%), never used MEDIUM in 2026. Cause found: the -0.123 s SOFT offset is an artefact of the wear-ordering constraint (free 2026 fit has SOFT +0.13 s slower; see `tire_model.py` 2026-10-25 docstring).
- No measurable cliff: residual slope beyond typical stint length is 0.00 s/lap/lap (2026), negative (2025), so a stint-life cliff would be tuned to team behaviour (tested k=0.05-0.4: +1 to +3 stop matches, noisy, worse winner gap; not shipped).

## Austin R18 (56 laps) — `austin_r18.txt`
Actual top 10: 7 one-stoppers (ANT M24/H32, NOR M16/H40, PIA M15/H41, RUS M22/H34, HAD S25/H31, LAW S25/H31, GAS S24/H32), 3 two-stoppers (LEC S15/M24/H17, HAM S14/M24/H18, VER S13/S18/M25). Optimizer before: #1 H27/S29 (one stop, lap 27), #2 M26/S30. After: #1 H28/M28, #2 H28/S28, #3 M28/S28 (a 0.2 s tie, show them as equivalent), best 2-stop +14.5 s. So stop count and compound set agree with the modal strategy; the pit lap (28 vs median 24) and the split do not: teams run a shorter MEDIUM and longer HARD (M15-24 / H31-41), the model has M and H wear tied. Shipped Austin params still carry 2025 caps with HARD C1 (cap 28), so the app is capped for HARD at 28 laps.
