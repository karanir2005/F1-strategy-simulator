# T-037 circuit trends in the race predictor (backend-engineer)

## What changed
- `backend/app/core/predictor.py`: `track_stickiness` can now use a per-race signal chosen by `PredictorConfig.stick_mode`: `rho` (old whole-field Spearman), `front` (Spearman over the top `stick_top`=8 starters), `pole` (1 if the winner started P1, else 0). New `stick_w_pre2022` (extra down-weight for 2018-21, default 1.0 = unchanged). Still a multiplier `exp(gamma*(s_c - s_global))` on the grid term, strictly-earlier races only, shrunk with `stick_k`=4.
- Shipped defaults: `stick_mode="pole"`, `stick_gamma=4.0` (was 0 = off).
- Tests: added `test_stickiness_signal_modes_no_leakage` (3 modes, scrambled target/future) and `test_pole_signal_is_winner_started_p1`. Adjusted `test_predictor.py` (explicit gamma 0 baseline) and `test_t012_qa.py` (history now carries the schedule, and `_truncate` keeps it, because the target's circuit must resolve; it is a calendar, not an outcome). Full suite: **344 passed**.

## Results (blend 0.75 PL / 0.25 grid-rank baseline as shipped; 20k sims)
Tuned on 2025 walk-forward only (gamma 2/4/8 per signal). Win LL, grid mode, 2025: rho0 0.0879; rho8 0.0894; front2 0.0889, front8 0.0945; **pole4 0.0847** (best, nll 1.014 vs 1.058); pole8 0.0855. Pre-2022 down-weight did nothing useful. Podium LL 2025 pole4 0.1961 vs 0.1939 (+0.002).

2026 walk-forward R2-17:

| setting | grid win LL | grid podium LL | grid winner NLL | prequali win LL | prequali podium LL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| gamma 0 | 0.0794 | 0.2083 | 1.110 | 0.1130 | 0.2454 |
| front 4 | 0.0787 | 0.2044 | 1.089 | 0.1130 | 0.2457 |
| **pole 4 (shipped)** | **0.0762** | **0.1990** | **1.040** | 0.1147 | 0.2459 |

Grid mode: pick is not the pole sitter in 0 of 16 races for every setting (2025: 0 of 23; only gamma 8 produced 1-2 non-pole picks, all wrong). Pole-start mean P(win) falls 0.557 -> 0.540 (2026), so the effect is a modest recalibration, not pick changes. Prequali hits are 6 for all settings. Prequali win LL is slightly worse (+0.0017 in 2026, +0.0008 in 2025), within noise but not free. I shipped because the criterion (2026 grid win and podium LL not worse) is met, but the gain is small and rests on a 0/1 signal from ~7-10 races per circuit.

Honest read: the pole sitter wins ~70% of the time, and with the model's own ratings the front-row starter is almost always still the favourite, so this does not give faster P2-P5 cars a pick-level edge. It lowers the pole sitter's win chance a little at overtaking tracks and raises it at Monaco-like tracks. Setting `stick_gamma=0` restores the old behaviour.
