## What I verified
- **Leakage, quali_ratio path (read code):** held-out race gets base = its own Jolpica quali best (pre-race info) x ratio. The ratio is `fit_quali_ratio` over the 9 other races only (`train = races where g != gp`). The held-out race's laps feed only the diagnostic `in_race_oracle_ratio_median` and the leaky rows. The LORO ratio is stable (1.0373-1.0392) while the held-out race's own ratio spans 1.013-1.070, so it is not tracking the answer.
- **Leakage, LORO tyre fit:** `tm.build_tire_model(laps=train_laps)` filters out `race_file == held-out`. `build_tire_model` loads from disk only when `laps is None`, so nothing falls back to the full set. The fuel coefficient is fitted inside the same call. Training-race `oracle_base` uses a model that was fitted on those same races (in-sample), which is not leakage of the held-out race. The existing no-leak test (corrupts a race and checks bit-identical output) covers this.
- **Reproduction:** `python validate_scorecard.py` is offline and ran in about 6 s. Its JSON is identical to the committed `latest.json` (regenerated 17:13, after the re-pull; diff over every non-pair field is empty, 190 pairs both).
